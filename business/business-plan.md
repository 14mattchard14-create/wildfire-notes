## Section 1: Executive Summary

### 1.1 Company Profile & Mission
CharredGuard Fire Risk Mitigation Services LLC ("CharredGuard") is a specialized wildland-urban interface (WUI) risk-consulting and home-hardening firm based in Southern California. The company provides professional, science-backed wildfire hazard inspections, customized mitigation blueprints, and minor on-site home-hardening services for single-family homeowners, HOAs, and property management groups.

Our mission is to safeguard homes, protect communities, and preserve property insurability across high-risk WUI corridors by transforming complex wildfire standards — such as Cal Fire regulations, IBHS Wildfire Prepared Home (WPH) frameworks, and NFPA guidelines — into actionable, affordable risk-reduction solutions.

### 1.2 The Problem & Market Opportunity
Across Southern California, particularly in North San Diego and Orange Counties, severe wildfire risks have triggered an unprecedented crisis in the residential property insurance market:

- **Insurance Non-Renewals:** Major carriers are dropping policyholders or drastically increasing premiums in WUI areas.
- **Complex Compliance Mandates:** Under California's "Safer from Wildfires" regulation (10 CCR § 2644.9), admitted property insurers that use wildfire risk scoring must offer premium discounts for documented wildfire mitigation measures. (See `legal-risk-notes.md` — this regulation mandates rate discounts, not guaranteed coverage or renewal; plan language should stay precise about this distinction.)
- **The Action Gap:** Homeowners frequently receive non-renewal notices or Cal Fire warnings but lack the technical expertise to audit their own properties or perform the specific, low-cost physical retrofits needed to satisfy underwriter requirements.

### 1.3 Service Offering & Business Model
CharredGuard bridges the gap between homeowners, regulatory guidelines, and insurance underwriters through a two-tiered inspection and direct-hardening model:

1. **Comprehensive On-Site Risk Audit ($500):** A detailed field evaluation using CharredGuard's custom digital assessment engine. The homeowner receives a photo-documented, prioritized mitigation blueprint covering Zone 0 (0–5 ft ember-resistant zone), Zone 1 (5–30 ft), Zone 2 (30–100 ft), and structural ember pathways.
2. **Guided Homeowner Self-Inspection ($200):** An accessible, remote-guided digital audit option for budget-conscious homeowners.
3. **Minor Hardening Add-Ons (<$1,000 aggregate):** Direct, handyman-level physical retrofits completed on-site — including installing 1/8" ember-resistant mesh over vent openings, clearing Zone 0 debris, applying non-combustible base flashing, and securing deck posts — executed strictly within California's AB 2622 minor-work licensing threshold. **Gated in the POAM until the legal/permit questions in `legal-risk-notes.md` are resolved.**

### 1.4 Target Market & Growth Strategy
- **Geographic Focus:** WUI communities in North San Diego County (e.g., Carlsbad, Escondido, Fallbrook, Ramona) and Orange County (e.g., Coto de Caza, Silverado Canyon, Laguna Beach) — primary areas; broader Southern California is in scope.
- **Primary Acquisition Channel (B2B2C):** Strategic referral partnerships with independent insurance brokers and real estate agents whose clients face non-renewal or closing delays due to wildfire risk.
- **Hyper-Local Direct Marketing:** Neighborhood "Radius Blitz" postcard drops, job-site lawn signage, and active Nextdoor service provider sponsorships. **Note:** signage/branding that could read as "holding out as a contractor" needs legal sign-off first — see `legal-risk-notes.md`.
- **Community Channels:** Local HOA presentations, Fire Safe Councils, utility wildfire fairs (SDG&E), and regional home shows.

### 1.5 Capital Requirements & Financial Highlights
CharredGuard is built on a hyper-lean, high-margin operating framework.

- **Startup Capital Requirement:** ~$2,500–$3,100, funding tools, NFPA CWMS certification, initial combined Errors & Omissions (E&O) / General Liability insurance, and California LLC registration ($800 FTB franchise tax + state filings).
- **Fixed Operating Burn:** ~$223/month (combined GL/E&O policy, custom tech infrastructure, Google Workspace).
- **Unit Economics & Profitability:** ~90%+ gross margin on inspection services. One $500 audit every two months covers fixed monthly overhead. Year 1 projections: $75,850 gross revenue, $57,246 net pre-tax profit.

## Section 2: Company Description

### 2.1 Ownership & Legal Structure
- **Legal Name:** CharredGuard Fire Risk Mitigation Services LLC
- **State of Formation:** California
- **Principal Operating Region:** Southern California — primarily North San Diego County and Orange County
- **Licensing & Regulatory Compliance:** Physical modifications offered directly by CharredGuard are limited strictly to non-structural, minor maintenance and retrofits where total contract value (labor + materials) remains under California's AB 2622 $1,000 minor-work threshold (B&P Code § 7159/§7048). Any major structural remodeling, full roof retrofits, or extensive tree logging are referred to licensed C-36/C-61 contractors. **This threshold figure is confirmed accurate (raised from $500 to $1,000 effective Jan 1, 2025) — see `legal-risk-notes.md` for the conditions attached to it that this plan doesn't yet address.**

### 2.2 Core Capabilities & Professional Standards
- **Standards Compliance:** Audits and report structures align with IBHS Wildfire Prepared Home (WPH) guidelines, Cal Fire Zone 0–2 Defensible Space regulations, and NFPA 1144 & 1140 standards.
- **Professional Credentials:** Operations and audits are spearheaded by an NFPA Certified Wildfire Mitigation Specialist (CWMS) *candidate* — certification not yet achieved as of this draft. Website trust badges are deliberately held back until certification is complete (tracked as a reminder for November 2026).
- **Risk Management & Insurance Protection:** Combined commercial GL + E&O policy (~$145/month). All client agreements include a liability limitation stating audits reflect observable conditions at the time of inspection and do not guarantee structural immunity from wildfire events.

### 2.3 Technology Infrastructure & Competitive Advantage
- **Proprietary Assessment App:** Fast, photo-rich mobile data collection mapped to IBHS/Cal Fire zones (this is the `wildfire-notes` app).
- **Custom CRM & Database:** Client intake, automated follow-ups, broker referral pipeline tracking.
- **Delivered Artifact:** Every $500 audit client receives a Wildfire Risk Mitigation Blueprint — hazard photos, urgency priority matrix, itemized action items, and a proof document for insurance brokers/carriers.

## Section 3: Market Analysis

### 3.1 Industry & Market Dynamics
- **FAIR Plan Growth:** Homeowners unable to secure private-market coverage are increasingly forced onto the California FAIR Plan; Southern California leads the state in policy volume.
- **"Safer from Wildfires" Mandate (10 CCR § 2644.9):** Admitted insurers using wildfire risk scoring must offer premium discounts for documented mitigation. **Confirmed real regulation — effective Oct 2022, insurer rate-plan compliance required by April 2023, expanded by AB 1 effective Jan 1, 2026.**
- **The Market Gap:** Homeowners lack a standardized way to prove mitigation compliance to underwriters — creating demand for certified, standard-aligned risk auditing.

### 3.2 Target Geographic & Customer Segments

**Target Geography:** North San Diego County (Carlsbad, Escondido, Fallbrook, Ramona, Valley Center) and Orange County (Coto de Caza, Silverado Canyon, Yorba Linda, Laguna Beach) as primary areas, within a broader Southern California service area.

**Primary Customer Personas:**

| Segment | Core Motivation | Service Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| WUI Homeowners Facing Non-Renewal | Received a non-renewal notice or price hike; needs proof of mitigation | Insurance carrier demand letter / broker referral |
| Proactive WUI Homeowners | Wants peace of mind, wants to claim discounts under CA law | Community wildfire awareness, HOA mandate, Fire Safe Council meeting |
| Real Estate Buyers/Sellers | Sale stuck in escrow due to insurance/AB-38 issues in a WUI zip code | Realtor referral during contingency period |

### 3.3 Competitive Analysis & Market Positioning
- **vs. Landscapers/Tree Services:** No structural ember-pathway knowledge, no insurance documentation, no building-hardening work.
- **vs. Traditional Home Inspectors:** No specialized NFPA 1144/1140 or IBHS training, no physical mitigation work.
- **vs. Licensed General Contractors:** High-overhead firms uninterested in $300–$800 retrofit jobs.

## Section 4: Service Line & Pricing Strategy

### 4.1 Service Offerings

1. **Comprehensive On-Site Wildfire Risk Audit ($500)** — 90-minute on-site evaluation by an NFPA CWMS-trained inspector. Covers Zone 0 ignition zone, structural ember pathways (vents, gutters, roof valleys, eaves, glazing, garage seals), Zones 1 & 2 (canopy spacing, deadwood, propane clearance). Deliverable: photo-documented Wildfire Risk Mitigation Blueprint + Proof of Inspection Certificate.
2. **Guided Homeowner Self-Inspection ($200)** — Remote, technology-assisted audit via guided mobile photo/measurement submission.
3. **Minor Home-Hardening Add-Ons (under $1,000 threshold)** — Vent ember hardening (1/8" stainless mesh), Zone 0 clearing (gravel/river rock, gutter guards), siding & post-base retrofits. **Held pending legal/permit sign-off — see `legal-risk-notes.md`.**

### 4.2 Pricing Architecture & Revenue Strategy
- ~90%+ gross margin on inspection services.
- The $500 inspection fee is billed separately from any physical hardening add-ons. **Open question flagged in `legal-risk-notes.md`: never split a single hardening job's real scope across multiple contracts to stay under the $1,000 threshold — that specific pattern is what CSLB targets as evasion, regardless of how the audit fee is billed.**

## Section 5: Operations & Technology

### 5.1 Technology Infrastructure & Proprietary Stack
Internally built stack (~$78/month combined: tech infrastructure ~$58/mo + Google Workspace ~$20/mo):
1. **Field Assessment Mobile Engine** — photo evidence mapped to 10 CCR § 2644.9 & WPH criteria, localized risk scores per zone.
2. **Custom CRM & Data Repository** — homeowner profiles, automated follow-up triggers for 75-day non-renewal deadlines (Ins. Code § 678 — confirmed real), broker referral pipeline.
3. **Deliverable Generation Pipeline** — Wildfire Risk Mitigation Blueprint + Underwriter Proof of Mitigation Certificate.

### 5.2 Operating Procedures & Service Delivery Workflow
1. **Phase 1 — Intake & Remote Analysis (15 min):** Property details submitted via portal; preliminary satellite sweep for canopy/slope.
2. **Phase 2 — On-Site Risk Audit (60–90 min):** Zone 0 → Zone 1 → Zone 2, plus structural ember pathways.
3. **Phase 3 — Blueprint & Reporting Delivery (within 24 hrs):** Blueprint to homeowner, Proof Certificate to referring broker.
4. **Phase 4 — Minor Hardening Execution (optional add-on):** Capped under the $1,000 minor-work limit per contract.

### 5.3 Quality Control, Safety & Risk Mitigation
- Explicit Limitation of Liability Waiver in every contract (see Section 9 — **flagged for attorney review** in `legal-risk-notes.md`).
- Combined GL + E&O insurance (~$145/month).
- Standard PPE; roof inspections from ground level/eave ladders with telephoto optics, not by climbing.

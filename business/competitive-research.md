# CharredGuard — Competitive Research (charred-guard-site)

**Captured:** August 2026. Scan of direct competitors in the wildfire home-hardening / defensible-space inspection space, used to identify gaps in the marketing site.

## Companies reviewed
- **Madronus Wildfire Defense** (madronus.com) — free self-inspect tier, $149 guided, $449 pro; heavy trust signals (NFPA badge, IBHS/Wildfire Prepared/United Policyholders partner logos, "10,000+ properties assessed" stat, reviews section).
- **Wildfire Mitigation Advisors** (wildfiremitigationadvisors.com) — Sonoma/Napa/Marin/Mendocino counties; homepage built entirely around the insurance crisis (names the Tubbs and Glass fires, specific neighborhoods, the FAIR Plan); has a blog and standalone topic pages (Defensible Space, Home Hardening, FAIR Plan, Assessment Cost, CWMS Credential); testimonials page; phone number in header.
- **Trident Inspection Group** (tridentinspectiongroup.com) — Orange County; built an entire page around AB-38 disclosure requirements; certification badges (Certified Master Inspector, IAC2); veteran/first-responder discount; next-day appointments.
- **FortressFire** — AB 38 / IBHS standard–based property assessment, not deeply reviewed.

## Gaps identified vs. charred-guard-site (at time of review)

1. **No trust signals** — no certification badges, no review count/star rating, no "X properties assessed" stat, no testimonials. Every competitor above leads with at least one of these. *(Deliberately held back — CWMS certification not yet achieved; tracked as a reminder for November 2026.)*
2. **Insurance underused** — competitors build their whole pitch around the insurance crisis (FAIR Plan, carrier documentation); the site only gestured at it once in the hero subhead. *(Addressed — homepage insurance section added.)*
3. **AB-38 not mentioned at all** — a free, concrete, legally-grounded hook (CA seller disclosure law for High/Very High FHSZ homes) that opens a real-estate/escrow customer segment. *(Addressed — woven into FAQ, Services, and Contact pages.)*
4. **No blog or topic pages** — thin site (originally 5 pages) vs. competitors with dedicated pages per topic (SEO + authority play).
5. **Local specificity missing** — site said "statewide" while competitors name actual counties, cities, and fires. *(Addressed — site now says Southern California, North San Diego County + Orange County as primary areas, matching the real business plan.)*
6. **No phone number** — every competitor has click-to-call in the header/hero; site only has email. *(Open — tracked as a reminder for November 2026.)*
7. **No free/low-commitment entry point** — Madronus offers a genuinely free self-inspect tier before paid options; cheapest CharredGuard option is $200/$500 flat.
8. **No discount program** — Trident offers veteran/first-responder discounts.
9. **No email capture** for not-ready-yet visitors, no Google Maps/service-area visual.
